# Sources and calculation notes

This independent example is based on NVIDIA's FY2026 annual Form 10-K and its contemporaneous full-year results release. It is a retrospective for the fiscal year ended January 25, 2026, using information available February 25, 2026. Later FY2027 quarterly results are outside its scope.

- [NVIDIA FY2026 Form 10-K, filed with the SEC February 25, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm): Item 7 results, liquidity, risk discussion, consolidated statements, Notes 2, 12, 14, and 16.
- [NVIDIA FY2026 full-year results release](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-2026/default.aspx): annual results and management commentary.

## Calculations and distinctions

- Data Center's share of total revenue is $193.737bn / $215.938bn = 89.7%, rounded to "about 90%." This is our calculation, not a separately reported metric.
- Annual Data Center **end-market** revenue was $193.737bn; Compute & Networking **reportable-segment** revenue was $193.479bn. The letter uses the former and does not interchange them.
- Cash from operations increased by $102.718bn - $64.089bn = $38.629bn, rounded to $38.6bn.
- Property/equipment/intangible purchases were $6.042bn in the cash-flow statement, rounded to $6.0bn. Net investing cash outflow was $52.228bn and includes other transactions.
- Shares repurchased were 282 million for $40.4bn under the 10-K's capital-return disclosure. Cash paid for repurchases in the cash-flow statement was $40.086bn; timing and presentation differ, so these figures should not be treated as identical.
- Cash dividends paid were $974m, rounded to $1.0bn. Research and development expense was $18.497bn, rounded to $18.5bn.
- Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities were $62.556bn, rounded to $62.6bn. Cash and equivalents alone were $10.605bn.
- The $95.2bn supply and capacity figure combines outstanding inventory-purchase and long-term obligations. Note 12 says substantially all is expected to be paid through fiscal 2027, while some agreements may be canceled, rescheduled, or adjusted before firm orders, potentially at additional cost.
- Note 12 separately reports $27bn of cloud-service commitments across multiple years, with $7bn due in fiscal 2027. It also lists other contingent investment and other commitments; the letter does not combine these as equally firm liabilities.
- FY2026 purchases of nonmarketable equity securities were $17.502bn, rounded to $17.5bn. The filing describes investments in private companies and infrastructure funds, including some AI model makers that buy NVIDIA products. The letter treats return and demand effects as uncertain.
- The Groq arrangement involved $13bn cash paid at closing and $4bn payable within one year, inclusive of imputed interest. It was a non-exclusive technology license and hiring arrangement, not an equity acquisition.

## Interpretation boundary

NVIDIA's filing attributes growth to AI and accelerated-computing demand and attributes the margin decline to the Blackwell transition plus the H20 charge. The letter presents these as management explanations. It does not estimate NVIDIA's intrinsic value, future share returns, or future AI demand. Its questions about customer returns and repurchase prices are analytical questions for owners, not asserted findings from the filing.
