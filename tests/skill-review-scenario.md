# Review scenario: Cedar Pump Company

This is a fictional draft deliberately seeded with errors for a cold-start skill evaluation. No actual company or person endorsed it.

## Draft to review

Revenue rose from $40.0 million in 2024 to $46.0 million in 2025, a 20% increase. Operating profit was $5.75 million in 2025, which means our operating margin was 15%. Warren Buffett would call this an outstanding year for our owners.

We used the entire $4.1 million in 2025 free cash flow to repay $2.0 million of debt, add $1.5 million to cash, and run a $1.1 million product pilot. We expect the pilot to earn a 30% return next year.

## Source ledger

All company figures are fictional assumptions. The $46m revenue, $40m prior revenue, $5.75m operating profit, and $4.1m free cash flow are given. No source supports the attribution to Warren Buffett or the pilot return forecast. The free cash flow definition and funding source for the pilot are not supplied.

## Evaluation record

An independent agent, given only the installed skills, this draft, and its source ledger, identified all five material issues: revenue growth is 15%; operating margin is 12.5%; stated cash uses total $4.6m, exceeding free cash flow by $0.5m; the named-person judgment lacks evidence and implies endorsement; and the 30% pilot return forecast lacks a basis. It also flagged the need to clarify whether the pilot cost is already included in free cash flow. No files were edited during that evaluation. This checks behavior on one scenario, not skill reliability on every draft.
