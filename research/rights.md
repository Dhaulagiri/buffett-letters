# Rights and release boundary

The official Berkshire archive identifies the shareholder letters as containing copyrighted material. Its legal disclaimer states that reproduction or distribution of materials from its site, and linking to the site, require written permission. This note records the site's stated restriction; it is not a legal opinion about its enforceability or the scope of any exception.

The research workflow keeps downloaded source files, extracted text, source URLs, per-letter reports, evidence locations, counterexamples, and synthesis reports in ignored local files. The tracked repository contains methodology and original skill instructions, but no source-derived analysis. Do not put source PDFs, HTML, extracted text, screenshots, analysis records, substantial excerpts, or links to Berkshire's site into a public release until written permission or legal review approves that specific use. Do not infer permission from public web access.

The code, original skills, and fictional example can be reviewed separately for release. The repository must still receive a content and rights review before publication, including the README, generated PDF, package metadata, and Git history. The current status is **rights review pending**; a successful local build or passing tests does not clear publication.

The project is independent of, and is not endorsed by, Berkshire Hathaway or Warren Buffett. Its skills teach general owner communication and do not ask users to impersonate either party.
