# Research boundary

This package was informed by a private qualitative study of annual shareholder letters covering fiscal years 1977–2024. The local workflow divides extracted text into indexed reading jobs, runs one private review per letter, consolidates results across eras, searches for counterexamples, and synthesizes original skill instructions.

The corpus, source URLs, per-letter reports, evidence locations, counterexamples, and synthesis reports are intentionally excluded from this package and from Git. The package contains no historical letter text, quotations, source links, or letter-level evidence identifiers.

The study supports qualitative guidance rather than frequency claims. It does not establish that any technique appears every year, that extraction preserved every table or visual feature, or that multiple independent reviewers agreed on every code. Embedded guest material also requires separate authorship checks.

Use these skills as an independent owner-update toolkit. Do not describe a generated letter as written, endorsed, or approved by Berkshire Hathaway or Warren Buffett. Do not imitate a named person's distinctive phrases, anecdotes, jokes, or persona. The user's business facts must come from its own authorized source ledger.
